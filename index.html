Why AIM exists

One club, two directions of impact.

Most student charity efforts point outward. AIM points both ways — inward, toward the people who keep PSU running, and outward, toward the communities around us — and grounds every project in a clear, trackable goal.

We started AIM because volunteering at PSU tended to mean one of two things: a single external donation drive, or nothing organized at all. There was no club treating campus welfare — the people and routines that hold university life together — as worthy of the same care as outreach beyond the gates.

So AIM runs both. Every initiative we plan is scoped, budgeted, and mapped to a Sustainable Development Goal before it launches — so "impact" is never just a word on a poster.
